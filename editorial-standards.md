InnovationLet Tech is part of InnovationLet, an independent technology publication run by the [F9XR Team](https://f9xr.org). These are the standards every story on this desk is held to. They apply to staff reporting, contributed analysis, and any material published under an InnovationLet byline.

## Sourcing and verification

- We go to primary sources first: release notes, standards documents, filings, court records, repositories, and on-the-record interviews. Where a claim comes from a secondary source, we link to it.
- We attribute facts to a named source wherever one exists. We do not use "sources say" or "experts believe" without identifying who is speaking and why they would know.
- When we cannot verify a claim, we say so in the story rather than presenting it as established. Corrections and uncertainty are stated in the text, not hidden.

## Bylines and accountability

Every story carries a byline. Desk-wide reporting is published under [F9XR News Desk]({{ '/author/f9xr-news-desk/' | relative_url }}), which means the piece was reported and edited by the Tech desk team. Contributed and guest pieces are labeled as such and name the contributor. We do not publish anonymous bylines on reported news.

## Corrections

- If we get something wrong, we correct it. Substantive corrections carry a note stating what changed and when.
- To report an error, email [hello@f9xr.org](mailto:hello@f9xr.org) with the article URL and the specific claim you believe is wrong. We aim to respond within three working days.
- We do not silently edit the meaning of a published story.

## Independence and funding

- The desk's reporting is editorially independent of F9XR Team's consulting and engineering work.
- InnovationLet is funded through its parent organization and, in future, through clearly labeled advertising and sponsorships. Sponsored or paid content is labeled at the top of the page and is not presented as editorial.
- We do not accept payment for coverage. Advertisers have no input into what we report or how we report it.

## Use of AI tools

- We may use machine-assisted tools for transcription, copy-editing, research triage, and translation. These tools assist the reporting; they do not make editorial decisions.
- No story is published without a human editor reviewing it. Facts, quotes and claims are verified against primary sources before publication.
- Any automated assistance that materially shapes a published piece is disclosed in the story.

## YMYL topics

Finance, economy and government coverage can affect readers' money, livelihoods and legal rights. On those desks we apply a stricter bar: named or clearly attributable sourcing, links to statutory or peer-reviewed material where it exists, a stated publication and review date, and an explicit note that our reporting is not financial, legal or investment advice.
