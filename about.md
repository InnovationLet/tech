InnovationLet is an independent technology publication run by the [F9XR Team](https://f9xr.org). This is the Tech desk: devices, software, platforms, and the engineering behind them.

## What we do

We read the primary sources so you don't have to. Release notes, standards drafts, filings, repositories, and the occasional angry mailing-list thread. Then we write the short version, with links, and without the marketing layer.

## Our standards

- **Primary sources first.** Every factual claim links to where it came from.
- **No vendor copy.** If a company says something is a breakthrough, we ask what it replaces.
- **Say what we don't know.** Uncertainty gets stated, not hidden behind confident phrasing.
