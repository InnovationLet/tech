InnovationLet is an independent technology publication run by the [F9XR Team](https://f9xr.org). This is the Tech desk: devices, software, platforms, and the engineering behind them.

## What we do

We read the primary sources so you don't have to. Release notes, standards drafts, filings, repositories, and the occasional angry mailing-list thread. Then we write the short version, with links, and without the marketing layer.

## How we report

- **Primary sources first.** Every factual claim links to where it came from.
- **No vendor copy.** If a company says something is a breakthrough, we ask what it replaces.
- **Say what we don't know.** Uncertainty gets stated, not hidden behind confident phrasing.
- **Named accountability.** Stories carry a byline. Desk-wide reporting runs under [InnovationLet Editorial]({{ '/about/' | relative_url }}), and we correct errors openly.

The full standards, including our policy on AI-assisted reporting, are on the [editorial standards]({{ '/editorial-standards/' | relative_url }}) page.

## Who is behind it

InnovationLet is owned and operated by the F9XR Team, a software and data engineering group. The publication's reporting is editorially independent of F9XR's client and product work. The Tech desk is written and edited by the InnovationLet editorial team.

## How we are funded

The publication is funded by its parent organization and, going forward, by clearly labeled advertising and sponsorships. We do not accept payment for coverage, and advertisers have no say in what we report. Sponsored content is labeled at the top of the page.

## Corrections

We correct mistakes. To report one, email [hello@f9xr.org](mailto:hello@f9xr.org) with the article URL and the claim in question. See the [editorial standards]({{ '/editorial-standards/' | relative_url }}) for how corrections are handled.
