We correct the record when we get something wrong. This page is our public register of corrections and the process behind them.

## How to report an error

Email [hello@f9xr.org](mailto:hello@f9xr.org) with the article URL and the specific claim you believe is wrong. We aim to respond within three working days.

## How we handle corrections

- Substantive corrections carry a note on the article stating what changed and when.
- We do not silently edit the meaning of a published story.
- The full policy sits in our [editorial standards]({{ '/editorial-standards/' | relative_url }}).

## Corrections register

No corrections have been logged for stories published on the InnovationLet Tech desk to date. New entries appear here with the date, the article, and a short description of what changed.
