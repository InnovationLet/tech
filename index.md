InnovationLet Tech is the hardware and software desk of the InnovationLet network. We cover the devices people actually buy, the platforms they build on, and the engineering trade-offs that never make it into a launch keynote. No spec-sheet theatre, no vendor copy. Just what changed and why it matters.

## What we cover

- **Devices**: phones, laptops, silicon, and the supply chains behind them.
- **Software**: frameworks, languages, developer tooling, and the open-source projects that move the industry.
- **Platforms**: cloud infrastructure, operating systems, and the standards that hold them together.
- **Engineering**: architecture case studies, performance work, and the decisions teams make under real constraints.

## How we report

We follow primary sources first: release notes, filings, standards documents, and repositories. When we cite a claim, we link it. When we don't know something, we say so. The desk publishes daily, with a longer brief each week for subscribers.

## Frequently asked questions

**What does InnovationLet Tech cover?**
Devices, software, platforms, and engineering practice. Every story is filed under one of those four areas.

**How often is it updated?**
The desk publishes daily during the week. Subscribe to the newsletter for a weekly round-up.

**Can I pitch a guest post?**
Yes. Send a short pitch to hello@innovationlet.com. We review submissions weekly and reply to every one.

**Where can I find the other categories?**
The full network covers artificial intelligence, space, finance, startups, economy, governments, and gaming.
