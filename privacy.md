This policy explains what information InnovationLet Tech collects when you visit this site, why we collect it, and the choices you have. InnovationLet Tech is part of InnovationLet, run by the [F9XR Team](https://f9xr.org).

## Information we collect

- **Usage data.** We use privacy-respecting analytics to understand which stories are read and how people find them. This may include pages viewed, referring source, approximate location derived from IP address, device and browser type, and timestamps.
- **Information you send us.** If you email us or submit a pitch, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message. We use it to reply and to follow up on your submission.
- **Newsletter.** If you subscribe, we store your email address in order to send the newsletter. Every issue includes an unsubscribe link.

We do not knowingly collect more than we need, and we do not sell your personal information.

## Cookies and similar technologies

- We use essential cookies and local storage where needed for the site to function.
- If analytics or advertising tools set cookies, those cookies are governed by the policies of the providers that set them. Where required by law, we ask for your consent before setting non-essential cookies.

## Third parties

We may use third-party services for hosting, analytics, email delivery and, in future, advertising. These providers process data on our behalf or, for advertising, as independent controllers under their own policies. Links to external sites are not covered by this policy; check the destination site's own privacy terms.

## Advertising

If we display advertising in future, ad partners may use cookies or device identifiers to serve and measure ads. Any advertising that relies on personalized data will be disclosed here and, where required, gated behind consent.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, and to object to certain processing. To make a request, email [hello@innovationlet.com](mailto:hello@innovationlet.com). We will respond within the time required by applicable law.

## Data retention

We keep usage data only as long as it is useful for understanding readership, and email correspondence only as long as needed to handle your request or submission.

## Changes

We may update this policy as our tools change. Material changes will be reflected in the "last reviewed" date at the top of this page.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: [hello@innovationlet.com](mailto:hello@innovationlet.com).
